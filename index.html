Privacy Policy — SMS Notifications

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This privacy policy covers SMS text message notifications sent by Kiyoshi Casey ("we", "us") through our automated project-monitoring systems.

What information we collect

We collect the mobile phone number you provide when opting in to receive SMS notifications. No other personal information is collected through the SMS program.

How we use it

Your mobile number is used solely to send you transactional SMS notifications you requested — automated project analysis summaries, system health alerts, and approval requests from our monitoring systems.

Mobile number non-sharing statement

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in consent data to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or any other purposes. Your number is used only to deliver the notifications you opted into.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You can expect approximately 2–10 messages per week, depending on system activity and alert conditions.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile phone service plan. Please contact your wireless carrier for details.

Opt-out and help

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at the email below.

Contact

Questions about this policy: kiyoshi.casey@gmail.com
